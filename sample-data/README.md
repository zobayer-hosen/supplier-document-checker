# Sample files for Task 1 (supplier documents)

All companies and people here are fictional.

- `supplier-pack-01.pdf` to `supplier-pack-10.pdf`: one supplier quotation with its terms and conditions each.
- `purchase-request.csv`: what the company asked for, with the maximum unit price for each item.
- `company-rules.md`: the rules your system must apply.

Each pack is for one item from the purchase request. Decide each pack separately.
